abstract: >-
  SpatialDex learns bimanual dexterity from RGB-only human videos by reconstructing scene geometry and hand motion and
  aligning them at a shared scale. Pre-trained on 1M human-video clips and fine-tuned with a few robot demonstrations,
  it outperforms the strongest baselines by 38.3 points on six real-world bimanual tasks and by 16.5 points on nine simulated tasks.
