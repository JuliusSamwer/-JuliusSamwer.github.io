abstract: >-
  <p>Can a world model learn <em>where</em> to abstract? With model and planning budget held fixed, a flat latent world
  model with sampling MPC goes from 100% to 5% success as the optimal path grows from 2 to 12 cells, while a two-level
  planner over learned macro-actions still reaches the goal (figure). Zeus asks whether the boundaries of these
  abstractions can <em>emerge</em> at decision points under planning pressure, instead of being imposed by hand.</p>
  <p><b>Paper 1</b> takes this to pixels, comparing flat, HWM-style and two-level JEPA world models on a FourRoom
  task with a moving distractor and probing what each level of the hierarchy encodes.</p>
